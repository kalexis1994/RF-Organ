Microphone placement and rotor stops

Placement is in the units a tape measure uses. A stop angle of 360 degrees is the documented "Rnd" setting, which stops that rotor somewhere new each time. The rotors are belted to alternating-current motors, so the supply they run from sets every speed they reach: the same cabinet on 50 Hz turns at five sixths of what it is rated for. Sub Bass is the woofer's own unmodulated output, which never enters a rotor and is heard mostly by the drum's pair of microphones.

Transformer calibration

Bipolar trims offset one transformer from the shared character: T1 carries the lower manual and pedals, T2 the upper manual, T3 the output stage.
